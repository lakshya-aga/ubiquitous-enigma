In this podcast, Huberman talks to Dr. Andy Galpin about the essentials of workout, how to get what you want out of the workout.
For any progress, you need progressive overload. To start first master the motion then speed and then the power.
Progressive overload can be achieved by tweaking a few variables. Choice of exercise (according to the speakers, these matter less than the deliberateness of the motion, and range), volume, intensity (maybe choose in terms of VO2 or heart rate percentage or max weight/strength percentage). It can also be in terms of frequency of workout.

For hypertrophy: focus on intensity rather than volume. For size take more rest between sessions. Optimal is 72 hours between muscle groups. 

For strength everyday workout is okay. For strength volume matters a bit more.

Incorporate breathe down to end your workout and in general any high giving activity to calm yourself and hold onto the rush for a prolonged period of time. General 3 minute breathing - breathe in for $x$ time and breathe out duration $2x$ Very underrated.

Mind muscle connection really help. Focusing on muscle while working out improves quality. Supersets can save time with very little harm to gains. Negligible unless a professional athelete.

Optimal rest is 2-4 minutes not 1-2.
