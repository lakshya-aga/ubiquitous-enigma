Elliptical copulas are induced from elliptical multivariate distributions.

Gaussian (normal) copula:
- Dependence described by correlation matrix.
- No tail dependence in extremes.
- Simple and computationally convenient.

Student's t copula:
- Adds symmetric tail dependence.
- Better when joint extreme events are observed.
- Controlled by correlation + degrees of freedom.

Practical point:
Use Gaussian for baseline simplicity, switch to t-copula when extreme co-movement is materially underestimated.
