
## Theme 1

Chapter 1 lays out the premise of Nero, w cautious and intellectually curious trader. Although certain in his methods, he gets waivered by someone around him, doing visibly better even though he knows that a lot of his counterpart's success is attributable to randomness.
A saying Aimad Taleb during my time at Astignes said aligns with this -> there are no old and bold traders, because the bold ones rarely survive the randomness to get to be old (no they do not seem to be related)

Key takeaway: When seeing success, try to model the probability of things changing and what would success have looked like then.
## Theme 2

Always lookout for survivorship bias. A good rule of thumb would be to sniff out how many started out -> billion monkeys on billion typewriters for billions of hours -> one will produce the Iliad. But if 1 out of 10 monkeys does the same, we might be forgiven to believe that the monkey really might be special
