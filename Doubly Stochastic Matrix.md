The doubly stochastic matrix is a matrix where rows and columns both sum to 1. eg.
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
0.896 & 0.044 & 0.060
\\
0.072 & 0.869 & 0.059
\\
0.032 & 0.087 & 0.880 \\

\end{bmatrix}
$$
Generally achieved through iterative normalisation of rows and columns
- Normalise each row
- Normalise each column
- Iterate till convergence
