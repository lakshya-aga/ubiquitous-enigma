Very short and simple compilation of basic ideas of Black holes. Some funny history is also there such as French going against the name Black Hole - trou noire because of it being obscene.
Interesting theories are presented. What can be inferred from a black hole - spin, charge and mass.
Does black hole retain information? As much as catching ash when a book is burnt.
Hawking radiation is presented as a theory for a potential paradox of Black holes. Black holes by nature, suck everything in and do not let anything escape. If that is the case, how do black holes end? Hawking radiation is the radiation that Black Holes emit from the edge of the event horizon that leads to its decomposition.

How to check spin?

Doppler effect coupled with event horizon light emission.
