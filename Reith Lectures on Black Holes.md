Very short and simple compilation of basic ideas of Black holes. Some funny history is also there such as French going against the name Black Hole - trou noire because of it being obscene.

Does black hole retain information? As much as catching ash when a book is burnt.
Hawking radiation is presented.

How to check spin?

Doppler effect coupled with event horizon light emission.
