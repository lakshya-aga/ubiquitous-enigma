The book is absolutely fantastic to get a glimpse of how our understanding of the universe evolved.
It starts off with some theories primarily based in supernatural powers stating the geocentric theory. This was later replaced by the helio centric theory.

How did we know about other galaxies?
Hubble measured the brightness of stars which is dependent on distance and luminousity. For certain stars assuming lumosity to be the same, distances for each was painstakingly calculated. When distances seemed to be chunky, we concluded clusters of stars or galaxies.

How did we discover universe was expanding?
Observing stars, we can see the Doppler effect. Because the light is red, we can say that the stars are moving away. Since all galaxies are moving away, we either belive that we are the centre of expansion (which is unlikely) or the other galaxies are also getting the same view.

So any 2 points are moving apart but there is no centre of expansion.


Theory or relativity by gravity:

Light bends around heavy objects like the sun. As spece time gets a curvature around such objects. So light loses energy in high gravity which means light can be imagined to have slowed down. But withing the same gravity, the observer sees the same speed of light. So between 2 events, one with lower gravity will see more time pass by.
