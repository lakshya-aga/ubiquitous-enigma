
General property: Random variable that continues with one failure outcome until success
Example: No. of coin tosses to get a heads in coin flip or number of dice rolls till first 6




Expected value calculation is trivial
$$
E[X] = \sum_{i=1}^{ \infty} X.P(X)
= \sum_{i=1}^{ \infty} X . (q)^{i-1}.p
$$
