A vine copula builds high-dimensional dependence using cascades of bivariate copulas.

Core idea:
- Decompose a \(d\)-dimensional copula into pair-copula building blocks.
- Organize dependencies through a sequence of trees (C-vine, D-vine, R-vine).

Why useful:
- Flexible tail dependence across pairs.
- Handles heterogeneous dependence structures better than a single global copula family.

Tradeoff:
- More flexible than elliptical copulas.
- More parameters and model-selection complexity.
